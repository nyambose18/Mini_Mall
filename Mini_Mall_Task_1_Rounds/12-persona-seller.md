# Lerato Mokoena · Seller
Fictional profile, age 36, electronics shop owner in Germiston. Uses a phone between counter sales and handles detailed listings on a laptop when needed.

| Core goal | Pain point | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| List accurate parts | Similar-looking modules differ in voltage and connectors | Create and edit listings with model, voltage, connector and compatibility fields |
| Keep online stock accurate | A counter sale can leave an online listing showing stale stock | Quick stock editing and an out-of-stock purchase block |
| Dispatch orders she can fulfil | Pending EFT and approved cash on delivery require different dispatch decisions | Payment and shipment statuses, tracking entry and access to refund requests |

“I need to know which orders I can pack today.”
