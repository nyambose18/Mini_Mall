# Priya Naidoo · Administrator
Fictional profile, age 34, marketplace operations coordinator in Durban. Reviews detailed cases on a laptop and checks urgent queues on a phone.

| Core goal | Pain point | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Keep the catalogue accurate | Missing voltage or model information can make a listing misleading | Review listings and delisting requests, record decisions and request corrections |
| Resolve order and refund exceptions | A delivery issue can look like a payment issue when statuses are combined | Filter orders by payment and fulfilment, review dispatch and refund evidence, record decisions |
| Prepare weekly sales summaries | Pending EFT, unpaid cash-on-delivery orders and refunds can distort the totals | Sales dashboard with date and seller filters and separate rand totals for paid sales, pending payments and refunds |

“I need to see what happened before I make a decision.”
