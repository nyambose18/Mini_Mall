# Thabo Dlamini · Buyer
Fictional profile, age 21, TVET robotics student in Johannesburg. Shops on a phone and buys parts for scheduled practicals.

| Core goal | Pain point | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Find a compatible part | Similar sensors may have different connectors or voltage requirements | Browse, model search, specification details and add to cart |
| Stay within budget and receive parts before class | Shipping costs and delivery dates can change which item he chooses | Cart totals in rand, postcode delivery estimate in working days, order placement and EFT or eligible cash on delivery |
| Know where his parcel is and resolve problems | A late or faulty item can disrupt a practical | Order history, tracking, receipt confirmation and item-level refund requests |

“Will this work with my board, and can I get it before Friday?”
