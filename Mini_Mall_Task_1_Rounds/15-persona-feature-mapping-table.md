# Round 5 · Persona–feature mapping

| Feature | Seller | Buyer | Administrator |
|---|---|---|---|
| Create and edit listings | Similar modules need clear specifications | Needs compatibility details | Checks model and voltage |
| Listing and delisting review | Needs correction feedback | Needs accurate active catalogue | Owns approval and delisting decisions |
| Browse and search | Provides searchable model data | Shops by board and part model | Reviews catalogue data |
| Stock and cart | Updates quantities after counter sales | Needs available items | — |
| Checkout and order placement | Receives orders with item quantities | Needs rand total and delivery estimate before ordering | Tracks order records |
| EFT and cash on delivery | Needs dispatch eligibility | Chooses EFT or eligible cash on delivery | Monitors payment exceptions |
| Shipping and tracking | Packs, dispatches and enters tracking | Checks arrival against practical date | Reviews dispatch exceptions |
| Confirm receipt | Sees fulfilment progress | Confirms the parcel arrived | Reviews order timeline |
| Refunds | Views item and reason | Requests refund and tracks decision | Reviews evidence and decides |
| Sales statistics | — | — | Needs paid, pending and refunded totals |

The profile ages are illustrative card metadata. Location sets the scenario for a postcode-based delivery estimate, while estimated dates remain route-specific. Remove unsupported personal habits and use observed interface behaviour to validate mobile workflows. The buyer’s compatibility concern supports required technical listing fields shared by the seller, buyer and admin screens.

A persona turns a specific user need into a feature or a design trade-off the team can evaluate.
